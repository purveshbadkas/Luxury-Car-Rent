FAQs

1. Is there a speed limit?

Rented cars are allowed up to 100 km/hr. However it is 80 km/hr in a few cities where some cars might be equipped with speed governors as per government directives. Rented cars strictly advises to follow local speed limits.

2. Can I extend/ cancel/ modify?

Yes, extensions are possible subject to availability & charges. Cancellations & modifications will attract nominal charges as per our policy.

3.Do you provide insurance coverage for rented vehicles?

Yes, all our rental vehicles come with comprehensive insurance coverage. However, please review our insurance policy for details on coverage limits and any applicable deductibles.

4.Is there a minimum rental period for your vehicles?

Our minimum rental period is 24 hours. However, we offer flexible options for extended rentals based on availability and customer needs.

5. Booking criteria & documents?

Min. 19 years old, have valid original government ID (Aadhar, Passport, or PAN only) and a valid driving licence for “Light Motor Vehicles”, which is min. 1 year old at the time of starting the trip
